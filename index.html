How we work with you

Every project follows the same four steps, whether it is a simple website or a full app.

  1. Listen

    We visit or call you to learn how your business works and what slows you down.

  2. Design

    We sketch the screens and show you before writing any code, so you can change your mind cheaply.

  3. Build and test

    We build in small parts and test each one, so problems are caught early.

  4. Launch and support

    We put it online, train your staff, and stay available for fixes and updates.
