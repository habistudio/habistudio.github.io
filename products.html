Our products

Five products, each led by one of our developers. Every product can be adjusted to fit your business.

Tala

Sales and inventory dashboard

Tala (Filipino for “star” and “record”) shows a small business owner exactly what sold today, what is running low, and how this month compares to last month, all on one screen.

  • Daily sales summary and charts
  • Low-stock alerts before items run out
  • Export reports to Excel or PDF
  • Works on laptop and phone browsers

From ₱499 per month

Product lead: Marilla Ignacio

Pila

Online queue and appointment system

Pila (“queue”) lets clinics, barangay halls, and service centers take numbers and appointments online, so people wait at home instead of standing in line for hours.

  • Book a slot or get a queue number online
  • SMS reminder when your turn is near
  • Admin screen to call the next person
  • Daily report of people served

From ₱799 per month

Product lead: Alexander Susana

Biyahe

Commute planner mobile app

Biyahe (“trip”) helps commuters plan jeepney, bus, and train routes across Metro Manila, with estimated fares and travel times that still load when the signal is weak.

  • Step-by-step routes with fares
  • Saved routes work offline
  • Reports from other commuters on delays
  • Free to download for Android

Free, with optional ad-free upgrade

Product lead: Rimar Joe Reynado

Tindahan Kit

Ready-made online store website

Tindahan Kit gives home businesses and sari-sari stores a simple online shop in one day: a product catalog, an order form, and payment by GCash or cash on delivery.

  • Mobile-friendly store design
  • Add products with a photo and price
  • Orders sent straight to Messenger or email
  • Your own web address

One-time setup from ₱3,500

Product lead: Marjorie Canaleja

Aral

Class records and grades portal

Aral (“study”) replaces paper class records for small schools and tutorial centers. Teachers encode grades once, and parents and students check them online.

  • Automatic grade computation
  • Attendance tracking
  • Parent and student login
  • Printable report cards

From ₱30 per student per year

Product lead: Lovechi Cabros
